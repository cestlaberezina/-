In this work, pauline explores the [[act]] of [[touch]] as both a physical and [[philosoph|philosophical]] encounter. Through the [[tactil|tactile]] [[process]] of [[sculpt|sculpting]], the artist [[question|questions]] who is touching and who is [[receiv|receiving]], pondering the [[space]] [[between]] them, the [[depth]] of the touch, and what [[remain|remains]] after it. the act of touch becomes a [[dynamic]] [[exchange]], one that [[trans|transcends]] the [[surface]] of the [[material]].

The artist’s [[attent|attention]] [[shift|shifting]] away from the surface to the internal dynamics — the points of [[tension]], [[void|voids]], and centers that reveal themselves as the process [[unfold|unfolds]]. there is an inherent [[resist|resistance]] in the material, sometimes stronger than the artist's [[intent|intentions]], creating a [[dialogue]] where the artist is not the sole actor but a [[listen|listener]] and follower, [[yield|yielding]] to the clay’s own will. Two sculptures, unprocessed brili clay, glaze, fired at 1050℃. Exhibited at [[earthern]]. Tbilisi, Georgia, 2021.
