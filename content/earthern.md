This [[exhibit|exhibition]] took place in March 2020 at Fotografia Gallery in [[tbilisi]], following several months of re[[search]] and [[art|artistic]] [[work]] from august 2019 to march 2020.

The project centered around [[earth]] — both as a [[medium]] and a [[concept|conceptual]] framework, [[engag|engaging]] with [[loc|local]] re[[source|sources]], [[environment|environmental]] concerns, and cross-[[disciplin|disciplinary]] [[cult|cultural]] [[integr|integration]]. The works explored the [[transformation]] of [[raw]], [[site-specific]] elements into artistic [[express|expression]], bridging fine art, embodiment practices, and activism.

The exhibition included:
- [[sculpt|sculptures]] made from [[wild]] [[clay]] sourced in Ajara and [[soil]] from the Rioni valley
- a [[film]] examining the [[eco|ecological]] and [[social]] [[impact]] of the Namakhvani dam
- a [[happenning]] incorporating soil from Namakhvani, [[merg|merging]] [[movement]], [[matter|materiality]], and [[protest]]. recorded and screened throughout the whole exhibition
- a clay-on-canvas piece as a result of the happening

displayed works: [[brili]], [[rv sculpture]], [[rv happening]], [[content/uplistsikhe]], [[idbfm]], [[imiom]], [[palpation]]. additionally, the exhibition presented a guest work: a documentary on namakhvani by marina kaganova and leo descristoforo.
