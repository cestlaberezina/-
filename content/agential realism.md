[[social constructivism]]
karen barad:  Instead of assuming that objects or people exist first and then interact, agential realism argues that things emerge _through_ their "intra-actions" with each other. Barad develops the notion of **intra-action** to emphasize that entities do not exist as separate individuals before they relate—they are formed through their connections, which leaves the problem of iteration unsolved. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agential_realism)
