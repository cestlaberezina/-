pauline pelagia berezina, formerly known as pauline garbbha (b. 1994, [[pskov]], russia) is a inter-, trans- and [[undisciplinary]] artist. her research interests are related to [[art|artistic]] re[[search]], [[somatics]], [[site-specific|site-specificity]], [[phenomen|phenomenology]], [[griefwork]] and the [[environment]]. her artwork is focused on carrying the real of the [[landscape]] and [[catastroph|catastrophe]] by means of [[photograph|photography]] and [[performance]], and moves between the traditions of performance art, photo art and environmental art. in exile since 2020, currently in paris.

![[https://static.tildacdn.com/tild3433-3630-4536-b866-653233623962/about.png]]

her [[art|artistic]] ambition is to create [[space|spaces]] for [[resonan|resonance]] with the [[natur|nature]] inside and outside. in this [[pulsation]] [[between]] inner and outer [[world|worlds]] she sees a [[potential]] of [[politic|political]] [[act|action]]: [[become|becoming]] toward a deeply ecological [[exist|existence]].

unrehearsed and intuitive, her [[work]] doesn’t aim to resolve but to [[cataly|catalyze]] — an [[adventure]], a [[dialogue]], a [[mistak|mistake]]. shaped by [[poor]] [[theatre]], [[somatics]], [[psychoanalysis]] and [[phenomen|phenomenology]], pauline berezina works in [[mess|messy]], liminal dimensions where [[the imaginary]] & [[the real]] collide. 

>moving in the landscape through the body i am or [[other|others]] are is the overall theme for my artistic practice, which recently is focused on [[trauma]], [[griefwork]], [[environment|environmental]] issues and sorts of [[internal]] or [[external]] [[catastroph|catastrophe]], reinforced by means of [[phenomen|phenomenology]] and [[semantic|semantics]].

>i believe that [[war]] happens first and perpetually inside, fever is the body's way to [[pray]] and any [[word]] is a [[gesture]]. for me matters: [[question|questioning]] as an [[act|action]], [[collaps|collapse]] at the end of the step, [[simpl|simplicity]] of tears, what turns [[grief]] into [[beauty]], when people move their feet to let me [[pass]] to my seat in the theater, [[wander]], [[reverie]], [[heart]], how to [[hold]] open the door in which i stand, intercellular [[space]], how to go on, [[worship]], how the [[space]] is [[fill|filled]] with some kind of [[electricity]], how to [[walk]] in one's own unbought skin, how to [[resist]] the [[loss]] of [[wonder]], what to do when no one is to [[blame]], [[nausea]], removal of [[distan|distance]], when something does not begin, how to [[fly]] underwater, [[abode|abodes]].
