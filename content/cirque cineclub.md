In spring 2025, Pauline Berezina and [Antoine Depreux](https://www.instagram.com/antoine_depreux/)  hosted a [[cine|cineclub]] that quickly shifted from a simple film [[gather|gathering]] into an [[intim|intimate]] [[circle]] of inquiry and compassion. What began as an open invitation — “a weekly cineclub at our home… no level of expertise required, we are looking for personal and [[emotion|emotional]] [[exchange]]” — grew into a steady group exploring the [[fragil|fragile]] terrain of [[friend|friendship]], [[stigma]], [[family]], [[narcissism]], [[addict|addiction]], and the ways people shape and un[[set|settle]] one an[[other]]. The films were never the point; they were [[spark|sparks]].

The program moved between Jacques Tati, Pasolini, Trier and [[doc|documentary]] — a loose [[constellation]] under the theme of [[circus]]: "its [[joy]] and [[melancholie]], [[ficciones]] and [[politic|politics]] — [[profane]] and [[sacred]]; a [[myth]], a [[smoke]], [[adventure|adventures]] and [[prank|pranks]], [[trembl|trembling]], [[absurd]], [[cathar|catharsis]], [[shock]], [[charlatan|charlatanry]]". Pauline held the space, offering [[attent|attention]] exercises and [[facilit|facilitating]] the conversations so the room stay [[honest]], [[porous]], and [[safe]]. Antoine was holding the external [[border]]: technical moments, [[rule|rules]], and a an example of [[vulnerab|vulnerability]]. The cineclub became less a screening series and more a shared [[practic|practice]] of looking at life through cinema’s trembling [[edge|edges]].
